# khatt_engine

Read `PLAN.md` first: the owner's principles, the plan by stages, what was learned (with numbers), and
the log of examinations. Update it after every step.

- Principles: drawing tools must work for any logo; nothing fixed (every value from the logo, from a
  relation between its measures, or from a reference redrawn with the logo's pen); the six families
  of parts; the hand method (measure, structure from a reference, the logo's constraints, draw, look,
  correct) applied to every logo, not its results on one.
- Measure before and after every change with `python -m khatt.bench.full DIR` (real logos stay outside
  the repository: a folder of pictures and `texts.json`). Do not push a change that does not help.
- Memory is limited: one logo per process (`khatt.bench.full` does this), results cached on disk.
- Talk to the owner in Arabic.
